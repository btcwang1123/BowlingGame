## App 隱私權（App Store Connect 問卷）

- 「您或您的第三方合作夥伴是否從此 App 蒐集資料？」→ **否，我們不會從此 App 蒐集資料**
- 結果標籤：**未蒐集資料（Data Not Collected）**
- 依據：遊戲沒有網路連線、帳號、廣告或分析 SDK；存檔（`save.json`）與設定（PlayerPrefs）只存在裝置上，不會傳出去。Unity 引擎本身的 `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` 已包在 UnityFramework 內。
- ⚠️ 確認 Unity Player Settings 已關閉 Unity Analytics／Cloud Diagnostics（Services 視窗），否則要改填「診斷資料」。

## 審核備註（App Review Information › Notes）

```
This game is fully offline and needs no login.

Controls: on first launch choose "Swipe" (drag left/right to aim, flick up to throw) or "Motion" (hold the phone firmly, press and hold the screen with your thumb, swing your arm back then forward like a bowling swing, and lift your thumb at the bottom of the swing to release). Swipe is the quickest way to try every mode. Motion control uses the gyroscope/accelerometer only; no permission prompt is needed.

"Coins" are earned only by playing and spent in the in-game Pro Shop. There is no real-money purchase anywhere in the app.
```
