常見問題

體感模式怎麼投球？
握緊手機，拇指按住螢幕，手臂往後擺再往前揮，揮到最低點時放開拇指。揮得越快球越快，出手時手腕轉動可以打出曲球。

一直顯示「沒抓到後擺」？
按下螢幕後先把手臂往後擺，再往前揮，動作要完整。也可以到設定改用滑動操作。

進度會不見嗎？
進度存在你的裝置上，刪除 App 會一併刪除。

FAQ

How do I bowl in motion mode?
Hold the phone firmly, press and hold the screen with your thumb, swing your arm back and then forward, and lift your thumb at the bottom of the swing. Swing faster for more speed; twist your wrist at release for a hook.

It keeps saying it didn't catch my backswing.
After pressing the screen, swing your arm back first, then forward, as one full motion. You can also switch to swipe controls in Settings.

Will I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on your device and is removed if you delete the app.
